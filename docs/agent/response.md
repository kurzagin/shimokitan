## SHIMOKITAN Archival Insight: The Dual-Layer Resonance Model

**Status**: [PROPOSAL_V3]  
**Focus**: Structural distinction between "The Registry" (Artifacts) and "The Encounter" (Exhibits).

---

### 1. The Core Philosophy
In a professional cultural ledger, we must distinguish between **The Object** and **The Moment**. 

* **The Artifact (The Object)**: The "Canonical Registry" entry. It is the static, professional record of a piece of software (Game) or a specific recording (Music Cover).
* **The Exhibit (The Moment)**: The "Experiential Shard." It is the media layer (Trailer, Opening Movie, Teaser) that acts as the gateway to the Artifact.

> **Insight**: We do not archive data; we archive the *impact* of that data on a resident's life.

---

### 2. The Memory Paradox (Resolved)
The previous "Memory Lock" occurred because a Zine was tied to the *Object*. By allowing Zines to target the *Moment*, we create a **Cumulative Life-Log**.

* **Artifact-Level Zines**: These are "Global Memories." They represent a long-term relationship with a game or a series.
* **Exhibit-Level Zines**: These are "Flash Memories." They capture the high-energy resonance of a specific trailer or cinematic at a specific point in time.

**The Rule of Permanence**: A resident can possess one Global Memory (Artifact Zine) and infinite Flash Memories (Exhibit Zines), provided each Exhibit is unique. This prevents "Memory Fragmentation" while allowing the user's profile to grow over years of engagement.

---

### 3. The Heat Dynamics (Non-Leaking Logic)
To maintain a high-contrast, "Fresh" Discovery Feed without faking the legacy of an Artifact, **Heat must terminate at the target.**

* **No Derivation**: Exhibit Heat does **not** flow upward to the Artifact. 
* **The "Engine" Effect**: The Artifact remains a "Cold Registry" (0 Heat) unless it is directly interacted with. It is the **High-Heat Exhibit** that acts as the engine, pulling the user from the Homepage into the "Cold" Professional Ledger.
* **The "Truth" Filter**: This prevents a masterpiece trailer from making a mediocre game look like a "Top-Resonance" Artifact. The trailer wins the Homepage; the game loses the Registry.

---

### 4. Categorical Adaptation
The system must adapt its "Targeting Logic" based on the medium's nature:

| Medium | Primary Discovery Unit | Zine Target | The "Work" Label |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Games** | **Exhibit** (The Trailer) | **Exhibit** | The IP Name (*Genshin*) |
| **Music** | **Artifact** (The Cover) | **Artifact** | The Song Name (*Idol*) |
| **Anime** | **Artifact** (The Season) | **Artifact** | The Franchise (*Frieren*) |
| **Anime Media** | **Exhibit** (The OP/ED) | **Exhibit** | The Season Source |

---

### 5. The "Professional Ledger" Standard
If everything were an Artifact, the search results for a major IP would be a "Graveyard of Fragments" (85 results for one game). 

**The Insight**: 
* **Search** must return the **Artifact** (The Authority).
* **Discovery (Feed)** must return the **Exhibit** (The Energy).

This ensures that `SHIMOKITAN` feels like a **Professional Archive** when you are looking for information, but a **Living District** when you are looking for inspiration.

---

### 6. Summary Tenet
> **"Resonance is an aggregate of moments, but the moments must remain distinct to be remembered."**

The Exhibit is the spark that starts the fire; the Artifact is the hearth that contains it. By separating their heat and their Zine slots, we allow the "History of the District" to be recorded without cluttering the "Registry of the World."
