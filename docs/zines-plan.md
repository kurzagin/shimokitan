# The Shimokitan Content Architecture: The Resonance Model

To protect the emotional weight of a user's memory and solve the complex relationships between original IPs, cover songs, and promotional materials, the Shimokitan database relies on a strict four-tier hierarchy.

This model ensures that trivial engagement is filtered out, creators are properly credited, and cultural resonance is accurately measured without diluting the core experience.

## 2. The Shimokitazawa Record Shop Analogy

To visualize how these relationships function in practice, imagine a small, independent record shop:

* **The Work is the Wooden Divider:** It sits in the record bin and simply reads "Harumaki Gohan." You cannot listen to the divider; it just guides you to the right section.
* **The Artifact is the Vinyl Record:** It is the heavy, physical album you pull from behind the divider—like the *Witch Eva Original Pressing* or the *Artist X Cover Album*. This is the tangible object you take home and experience.
* **The Exhibit is the Packaging:** It is the hype sticker on the shrink wrap, the glossy liner notes, or the obi strip on the spine. It gives the record context and makes it a beautiful artifact, but nobody writes a diary entry about the packaging.
* **The Zine is the Handwritten Letter:** It is a note folded up and tucked inside the vinyl sleeve by a previous owner, detailing where they were in life when they first heard the album.

Every time a user writes a Zine, they are slipping a letter into the sleeve of a specific vinyl record (Artifact), not taping it to the wooden divider (Work) or the hype sticker (Exhibit).

## 3. The Flow of Resonance (Heat vs. Memory)

A critical function of this architecture is how it handles the separation of human memory from mathematical visibility.

### Isolation of the Zine

Zines are strictly isolated. If a user writes a Zine about Artist X's acoustic cover of *Witch Eva*, that Zine remains permanently attached to the Cover Artifact. It does not duplicate, transfer, or bleed over to Harumaki Gohan's original vocaloid Artifact. The context of the memory belongs exclusively to the trigger.

### Aggregation of the Heat

While the written memories remain isolated, the "Heat" (the mathematical weight of a Zine generated by the author's reputation) flows upward.

The total Resonance of a **Work** is calculated by aggregating the Heat of all its child **Artifacts**. This means an original composition can rise in deterministic discovery on Shimokitan because the community is actively engaging with its various covers, live performances, or adaptations, accurately reflecting its massive cultural footprint.
